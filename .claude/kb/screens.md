## quiz.tsx `Quiz`
Screen scrollKey = current ? index : status (no jump on answering).
- preparing: ScreenHeader "Spot the Scam" + centered (minH 360) PulseDots quiz + muted "Getting your practice messages ready…".
- AI failed or not done within 15s (per-build deadline) with images available → image-only quiz (no error UI; AI build continues/retries silently for next round). error (no images): header + LeafCard line {feedbackTitle "That didn't work." + bodyStrong error} + quiz "Try again".
- asking/answered: title header + row callout muted "Message N of 5" + 5 pills 16×10 (border 2 lineStrong; right safeInk fill; wrong scamInk fill; current unanswered accent border; hidden from a11y) · LeafCard line message: soft header strip (small muted kind, calloutStrong from) + body text p18, or image question: expo-image (accessible, role image, label alt, contentFit contain, width 100% + maxHeight 480 + alignSelf center, aspectRatio ?? 9/16) · asking: quiz large "It's a scam" + quizOutline large "It's not a scam" (gap 12) · answered (FadeIn gap 14): LeafCard safeTint/scamTint {feedbackTitle safeInk/scamInk "You got it."/"Not quite." header + bodyStrong "This one is (not) a scam."} + subheading "The sign to remember" + explanation + quiz "Next message"/"See my score".
- finished (FadeIn pt20 gap22): title + LeafCard line (py28 px22) {callout muted "Your score", display quiz "{score} of {n}", scoreLine} + quiz "Try again" (createQuiz) + quizGhost small "Read about AI Scams". scoreLine (score of items.length; image fallback quiz may be <5): all → "You caught every one. Those signs will help you when a real one arrives." · ≥60% → "Nicely done. Each round makes the signs easier to see." · else "Scams are built to fool people. Each round you practice makes the next one easier to spot.".
- announce "You got it."/"Not quite." on answered.
